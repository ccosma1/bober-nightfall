# Bober Nightfall mark

Metaphor: **a wooden stick-sword planted in a frost-cracked ice shard**,
with two beaver ear-bumps peeking from behind the blade — night raid
into the ice-woods. At 32px the read is **vertical sword + ice shard**,
not a face, not a hut, not logs, not a slingshot.

Shape: **full-bleed square field** of night purple. Primary silhouette
is a **short wooden stick-sword** (round pommel, thick blade, not a
rapier) standing upright, jammed into a **triangular ice shard** that
sits on a tiny frost bank. Two small **rounded ear-bumps** peek from
behind the upper blade (ears only — no square chocolate-bar head, no
buckteeth portrait). Optional tiny ice-blue sparkle on the shard, not
a moon. Not a circle-in-a-badge, not stacked horizontal logs, not a
snow-capped lodge mound.

Palette (4):
- Night purple `#3A2A6A`
- Sword wood `#5C3A1A`
- Ice `#A8C4E8`
- Fur / wood brown `#8B5A2B`
- (supporting) Bucktooth cream `#F4E6C3` frost bank,
  accent yellow `#F5C400` (tiny pommel tack, not a sling-V)

Forbidden (do not reuse):
- **Bober Yeet**: square chocolate-bar beaver FACE as the whole mark,
  yellow sling-V, giant buckteeth on a square head
- **Bober Dam Defense**: beaver peeking OVER stacked horizontal capsule
  LOGS, crescent moon in the corner, 32px read of log bars + teeth
- **Bober Frost Lodge**: snow-capped stick-lodge mound + hearth flame,
  hut/doorway, chimney fire
- **Orion / grok-crypto-trader**: constellation, stars, deep-space navy,
  cyan rim, nebula glow, stacked glow-orbs, rounded-square + inner
  glow-circle recipe
- **Lowlight**: iron lantern, peaked roof, keyhole glass, oxblood / amber lamp
- **ViaPass / VignetteRoute**: cream travel ticket, punch-holes, terracotta sun
- **ConstructCost**: invoice / calculator tiles
- CMC / MultiversX Bober coin art — do not paste or trace
- Same rounded-square badge + glow-circle recipe as any sibling
- Crescent moon (Dam owns that), sling-V, stacked dam logs, wallet/crypto
  glyphs, text, stars-as-constellation, lodge flame, hut doorway
